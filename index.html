A CS student building at the intersection of software and hardware

Hi, I'm Jhon Eric Arbas — a Computer Science student with a passion for building practical, real-world technology solutions. I enjoy the full spectrum of development, from designing clean user interfaces to integrating hardware systems through IoT.

My work spans across software development, web applications, IoT systems, and UI/UX design — disciplines I treat as complementary rather than isolated. I believe the best products are ones that are both technically sound and genuinely pleasant to use.
